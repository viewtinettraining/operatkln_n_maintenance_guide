<h1><strong><span align="center">Configurations</span></strong></h1><p></p><p><span align="justify">The Configurations tab is your central archive for all collected device outputs (typically backups of running‑config or startup‑config). You can browse directories, view raw files, download or compare snapshots, and—when ready—merge multiple captures into a single, consolidated file.</span></p><p></p><figure align="center"><img src="https://app.snazzydocs.com/storage/users/ucsRFoMgaUeUU6iR/docs/o16HcrWNNphk1dYg/images/IzrHdGro2M4apBy4Z9Y2.png" align="center"></figure><p></p><ol><li><p><strong>Browse your archive</strong><br>Click <strong>File Browser</strong> to open a hierarchical view of all configuration folders and files. Drill down into task names, subtasks or device‑specific directories to locate individual <code>.cfg</code> files.</p><p></p><figure align="center"><img src="https://app.snazzydocs.com/storage/users/ucsRFoMgaUeUU6iR/docs/o16HcrWNNphk1dYg/images/TahQXv63UxaXQI3ntgCP.png" align="center"></figure><figure align="center"><img src="https://app.snazzydocs.com/storage/users/ucsRFoMgaUeUU6iR/docs/o16HcrWNNphk1dYg/images/deAkevp0gL45LzMB3GLs.png" align="center"></figure><figure align="center"><img src="https://app.snazzydocs.com/storage/users/ucsRFoMgaUeUU6iR/docs/o16HcrWNNphk1dYg/images/GQChaXKTGLxwNS9iSfZz.png" align="center"></figure><p></p></li><li><p><strong>Search, Edit or Delete</strong><br>Back in the main list, use the <strong>Search</strong> icon (🔍) to preview a file inline, <strong>Edit</strong> (✏️) to rename it, or <strong>Delete</strong> (🗑️) to remove it from the archive.</p><figure align="center"><img src="https://app.snazzydocs.com/storage/users/ucsRFoMgaUeUU6iR/docs/o16HcrWNNphk1dYg/images/0gQTeC41DROskFX1Ddzh.png" align="center"></figure><p></p></li><li><p><strong>Download Selected Files</strong><br>Select one or more configurations via the checkboxes, then click <strong>Download Selected</strong> to bundle them into a ZIP for offline storage or audit.</p><p></p></li><li><p><strong>Compare Two Snapshots</strong><br>To spot differences between two captures, select exactly two files and click <strong>Compare Selected</strong>. A side‑by‑side diff viewer will highlight added, removed, or changed lines.</p><figure align="center"><img src="https://app.snazzydocs.com/storage/users/ucsRFoMgaUeUU6iR/docs/o16HcrWNNphk1dYg/images/aRr32fgoZpQVAE1LuBXL.png" align="center"></figure><figure align="center"><img src="https://app.snazzydocs.com/storage/users/ucsRFoMgaUeUU6iR/docs/o16HcrWNNphk1dYg/images/Fi72EjNsSiPv4LLpe41s.png" align="center"></figure><p></p></li><li><p><strong>Merge Multiple Captures</strong><br>Select two (or more) snapshots and click <strong>Merge Selected</strong> to open the merge editor. You’ll see each file side‑by‑side; after resolving any conflicts you can click <strong>Merge</strong> to create a unified <code>.cfg</code> file.</p><figure align="center"><img src="https://app.snazzydocs.com/storage/users/ucsRFoMgaUeUU6iR/docs/o16HcrWNNphk1dYg/images/36kPUwkbW21h8XhFss15.png" align="center"></figure><figure align="center"><img src="https://app.snazzydocs.com/storage/users/ucsRFoMgaUeUU6iR/docs/o16HcrWNNphk1dYg/images/YueuLTTPLzfhe8eX4MsJ.png" align="center"></figure><p></p></li></ol><p><strong>Tip:</strong> Use <strong>Compare</strong> before <strong>Merge</strong> to ensure you understand every change. Merged files will appear in the archive with a timestamped name for traceability.</p><p></p>
