# **<span align="center">About</span>**

> ⚠️ **Disclaimer**<br />
> The **Viewtify QoS** module—like Viewtimon—is normally deployed on Viewtinet’s purpose-built appliances. If you are an engineer from a partner or distributor, you generally will **not** need to perform low-level tuning or appliance-specific steps unless there is a commercial exception permitting installation on non-standard hardware.
> 
> In all other cases—such as engagements by Viewtinet Professional Services or Support—the procedures in the following sections explain how to install and optimize Viewtify QoS on approved platforms.
> 
> **Prerequisite:** Viewtify QoS requires a **running** Viewtimon installation before it can be deployed.

**Viewtify QoS** extends Viewtinet’s deep packet inspection (DPI) capabilities into an enterprise-grade traffic management engine. By classifying, prioritizing, and shaping flows in real time, it enables you to:

-   **Deep Packet Classification**<br />
    Leverage DPI to accurately identify applications, protocols, and metadata on every flow—even on non-standard ports or encrypted traffic.
-   **Bandwidth Allocation & Traffic Shaping**<br />
    Define granular traffic-shaping rules to allocate fixed or dynamic bandwidth shares per application, user group, or business service. Prevent any single flow from starving critical services during peak load.
-   **Priority Queuing & Scheduling**<br />
    Assign priority levels (e.g. real-time voice/video, mission-critical business apps, bulk transfers) and enforce strict or weighted-fair queuing to guarantee low latency and jitter for sensitive traffic.
-   **Real-Time Policy Enforcement**<br />
    Push policy changes instantly across all probes and collectors. Viewtify’s centralized dashboard provides live telemetry on bandwidth usage, queue depths, and dropped packets—so you can tune policies on-the-fly.

With Viewtify QoS, you gain complete, application-aware control of your network’s bandwidth—maximizing performance for mission-critical services while containing non-essential traffic.
